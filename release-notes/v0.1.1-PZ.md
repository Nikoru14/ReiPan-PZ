Mouse controls in play, and a smoother Camera Obscura with the
over-the-shoulder camera.

## New

- **Mouse controls**, as in MikuPan:
  - **Looking:** the mouse turns the over-the-shoulder camera and aims the
    Camera Obscura, within the same limits as the stick. The pointer is
    captured only while it looks, and is free in every menu and while the
    overlay's bar is up. **Press F1 to hide the bar** and the mouse looks
    around.
  - **Right button** raises the Camera Obscura: **Hold** (up while held) or
    **Toggle** (click to raise, click to lower), under **Options → Controls →
    Camera Obscura button**.
  - **With the Camera Obscura up:** left button is the shutter (held charges),
    middle button the lens shot, and the wheel changes film.
  - **Mouse look** and **Mouse sensitivity** are on the same page. The
    overlay's **Settings → Controls → Mouse** also moves each action to another
    button, side buttons included. Everything is saved under `[mouse]` in
    `reipan.ini`.

## Fixes

- **Raising and lowering the Camera Obscura** on a walk the game films with
  an event camera (the dream's graveyard path) no longer cuts to the event's
  fixed shot for the animation. The over-the-shoulder camera stays on Rei.

## Installing

This build is for **Windows (64-bit)**. Only the European version of the game
works: **Project Zero 3, SLES-53825**.

1. Extract the zip anywhere and run `ReiPan.exe`.
2. The first time, it asks for your own copy of the game: the **disc image
   (.iso)**, or **IMG_BD.BIN** with `ZERO3.FHD` from the disc in the same
   folder. It remembers your choice.
3. For 60 fps camera and motion, set **Options → Graphics → Extra frames → 1
   extra**. The game still runs at its own pace.

Settings, saves and `reipan.log` are kept in ReiPan's user folder (overlay:
**Settings → Open user data folder**). Please include `reipan.log` when
reporting a problem.
