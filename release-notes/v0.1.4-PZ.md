Fixes for Rei's hair, head and leg vanishing.

## Fixes

- **The back of Rei's hair went missing**, on the rooftop walkway among
  other places, and stayed missing for the rest of the night until a save
  was loaded.
- **Rei's head vanished for a split second**, and that was what took the
  hair with it.
- **Rei's leg vanished for a moment while petting the cat.**

All three had one cause. On the PS2, the square root of a number a hair
under zero gives a tiny number, and dividing by zero gives the largest
number. On a PC both give "not a number" (NaN). When two animation keys were
almost identical, rounding made one bone's blend take that square root. The
bone went NaN for a few frames, and with it everything hanging from it: her
head, or her leg. The spring that sways her hair then kept that NaN for good.
The game now calculates these the way the PS2 does. The same kind of
division by zero is fixed wherever it could happen: cloth, hair chains,
ghosts walking their routes, lights, effects and cameras.

## For troubleshooting

- **Draw inspector** (overlay: **Debug → Diagnostics / A-B → Draw
  inspector**): hides one draw at a time, a character's pieces or the effect
  sprites, says what each is and why any of its triangles were dropped, and
  writes the frame's draws to `reipan.log`.

## Installing

This build is for **Windows (64-bit)**. Only the European version of the game
works: **Project Zero 3, SLES-53825**.

1. Extract the zip anywhere and run `ReiPan.exe`.
2. The first time, it asks for your own copy of the game: the **disc image
   (.iso)**, or **IMG_BD.BIN** with `ZERO3.FHD` from the disc in the same
   folder. It remembers your choice.
3. For 60 fps camera and motion, set **Options → Graphics → Extra frames → 1
   extra**. The game still runs at its own pace.

Settings, saves and `reipan.log` are kept in ReiPan's user folder (overlay:
**Settings → Open user data folder**). Please include `reipan.log` when
reporting a problem.

The same fixes are in **ReiPan PS5 v0.1.2-PZ**, released separately.
