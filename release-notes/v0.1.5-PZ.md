Gyro aiming with recentering, smoother walking through camera changes, and
old saves that load again.

## New

- **Gyro aiming** (from ReiPan): while the Camera Obscura is raised, a
  controller with a gyro (DualSense, DualShock 4, Switch Pro, ...) aims it by
  being turned, alongside the stick and the mouse. **Options → Controls →
  Gyro** sets the sensitivity, yaw or roll to turn, and the inversions; the
  gyro's drift is learned whenever the controller lies still with the Camera
  Obscura down.
- **Gyro recenter behaviour** (same page): the right half of the touch pad
  with the Camera Obscura raised holds the gyro still while pressed, so the
  controller can be brought back to a comfortable angle, and then:
  - **Zero** (the default): the view stays where it is.
  - **Level**: the view tips back to level.
  - **Level and back to start**: it also turns back to where Rei faced when
    she raised the camera.
- **Graphics API** (from ReiPan): **Options → Graphics → Graphics API** picks
  Direct3D 12, Vulkan or the automatic choice; one that cannot start falls
  back to automatic.
- **Trigger rumble** (from ReiPan) on Xbox controllers' trigger motors, with
  its strengths in **Options → Controls → Rumble**.

## Fixes

- **Walking through a camera change no longer fights the over-the-shoulder
  camera.** Holding the stick through a door, a search or an event shot keeps
  Rei walking the way she was, so the cut never turns her round, but coming
  back to the over-the-shoulder camera that old direction used to stick until
  the stick was let go. Now it eases onto the camera's direction within a few
  frames, and steering the stick a new way hands it to the camera at once.
- **Old saves no longer crash when loaded** (from ReiPan), including saves
  made by another build or on another PC: a save kept a pointer that only
  meant something to the build and session that wrote it.
- **Vulkan no longer crashes at the title screen** (from ReiPan).
- **A crash now leaves `crash.log` and `crash.dmp`** in the user folder, with
  where it happened; please include them with `reipan.log` when reporting one.

## Installing

This build is for **Windows (64-bit)**. Only the European version of the game
works: **Project Zero 3, SLES-53825**.

1. Extract the zip anywhere and run `ReiPan.exe`.
2. The first time, it asks for your own copy of the game: the **disc image
   (.iso)**, or **IMG_BD.BIN** with `ZERO3.FHD` from the disc in the same
   folder. It remembers your choice.
3. For 60 fps camera and motion, set **Options → Graphics → Extra frames → 1
   extra**. The game still runs at its own pace.

Settings, saves and `reipan.log` are kept in ReiPan's user folder (overlay:
**Settings → Open user data folder**). Please include `reipan.log` when
reporting a problem.

The same changes are in **ReiPan PS5 v0.1.3-PZ**, released separately.
