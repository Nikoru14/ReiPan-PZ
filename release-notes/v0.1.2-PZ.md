A Scare camera setting for the over-the-shoulder camera, faster texture
streaming, and fixes for two black screens and the save thumbnails.

## New

- **Scare camera** (**Options → Camera**, or the overlay's **Settings →
  Camera**) decides what the over-the-shoulder camera does when the game cuts
  to an event camera while you can still walk -- a ghost appearing, something
  to look at, the dream's graveyard path. It replaces the old **Event
  cameras** switch (a saved **Original** carries over).
  - **Guided** (the default) stays over the shoulder and turns once towards
    what the event's shot frames. The right stick or the mouse takes over at
    once.
  - **Original** plays the game's own shot, as before.
  - **Off** stays over the shoulder and turns nowhere.
- **The overlay's bar** (F1) stays the way you left it from one launch to the
  next.

## Faster

- **Texture streaming.** The game sends its textures to the PS2's graphics
  memory every frame -- 2 to 3.5 MB in a room -- and ReiPan now writes them
  through precomputed swizzle tables instead of working out every texel's
  address, about 1.75 times as fast. The result is byte for byte what it was.

## Fixes

- **A black screen at the start of a cutscene** (in Hour I, opening the door
  to the first ghost fight): a sound-stream read could wait for ever, and the
  scene waited on it.
- **A black main menu after quitting a game**, or a scene's music and voice
  going missing: a sound stream stopped just after it started left a thread
  running that kept its slot busy, so nothing could play on it again. The
  menu's background movie and music use that slot.
- **Striped save thumbnails** on the load and save screens.

## Installing

This build is for **Windows (64-bit)**. Only the European version of the game
works: **Project Zero 3, SLES-53825**.

1. Extract the zip anywhere and run `ReiPan.exe`.
2. The first time, it asks for your own copy of the game: the **disc image
   (.iso)**, or **IMG_BD.BIN** with `ZERO3.FHD` from the disc in the same
   folder. It remembers your choice.
3. For 60 fps camera and motion, set **Options → Graphics → Extra frames → 1
   extra**. The game still runs at its own pace.

Settings, saves and `reipan.log` are kept in ReiPan's user folder (overlay:
**Settings → Open user data folder**). Please include `reipan.log` when
reporting a problem.

A first PlayStation 5 build of ReiPan is released separately as **ReiPan PS5
v0.1.0-PZ**.
